Here is a little example of mathematics on my blog.

The Pythagorean theorem says that, for a right triangle with sides
\(a\) and \(b\) and hypotenuse \(c\), we have:

$$
a^2 + b^2 = c^2
$$

For example, if \(a = 3\) and \(b = 4\), then:

$$
c = \sqrt{3^2 + 4^2} = 5
$$
